## Permission gate

Feishu media defaults to `permission-review-required`. Authenticated readability is not evidence of authorship, redistribution permission, or modification permission; an asset cannot be committed until those rights have explicit evidence.

JavaGuide media defaults to `asset-level-review-required`. The repository's Apache-2.0 license can cover original repository assets, but it does not establish that every embedded third-party image is owned by the repository or may be modified and redistributed. Each candidate therefore needs repository-path, author, original URL, license URL, attribution, and modification evidence before reproduction or adaptation.
