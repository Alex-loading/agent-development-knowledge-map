# Chapter Standard

## Purpose

Write a self-contained Chinese chapter that develops a usable mental model and prepares the learner for the lesson's quiz, interview questions, exercise, and next lesson.

## Fixed teaching progression

Use this progression across the chapter. Combine adjacent functions when necessary, but do not omit one without recording why.

1. **先修桥接**：connect prior knowledge to the current problem and name what the learner should already know.
2. **直觉模型**：give a compact analogy, contrast, or conceptual map; label the boundary of every analogy.
3. **准确机制**：explain the causal chain, components, inputs, outputs, and constraints precisely enough to correct the intuition.
4. **工程意义**：translate the mechanism into design decisions, observability, cost, latency, reliability, and failure modes relevant to Agent development.
5. **具体例子**：walk through at least one concrete scenario that applies the mechanism and exposes a decision point.
6. **常见误区**：state tempting but wrong claims and correct each one with its consequence.
7. **回顾与下一章**：compress the reasoning into recallable points and create an explicit bridge to the next lesson.

## Section and paragraph constraints

- Produce 4–7 substantive sections, excluding the introduction, recap, and next-step fields.
- Give each substantive section 2–4 short paragraphs. Keep one reasoning move per paragraph.
- Define every technical term at first use in plain Chinese, then use the precise term consistently.
- Start each section with the learner's question or conceptual job; end with the engineering consequence or bridge.
- Prefer a causal sequence or comparison table expressed as prose/data over a disconnected glossary.
- Separate stable mechanisms from implementation-specific or date-sensitive behavior.

## Teaching checks

- Verify that a learner can answer every quiz without memorizing the option order.
- Teach the reasoning pattern behind every interview short answer and anticipated follow-up.
- Give enough operational detail to complete the exercise deliverable.
- Avoid repeating the same definition across sections; refer back and deepen it.
- Keep examples concrete but do not present invented numbers, benchmarks, APIs, or source content as facts.
